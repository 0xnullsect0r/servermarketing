# Post for the official All The Mods Discord

**This is the single highest-value post in the entire plan.**

Measured 2026-09-20: the official All The Mods Discord has **177,035 members
and 59,120 online**, and its own public description says *"You can also get
support for our Modpacks and find a server to play on."*

For scale: all 43 publicly listed ATM10 servers combined have roughly 600
concurrent players. This one Discord has a hundred times that many people
online right now, and they are all, definitionally, ATM players.

## Before posting: the ten minutes that matter most

I could not join the Discord, so **I do not know what its
server-advertising channel is called, whether it has one, or what its
cooldown is.** The public description implies such a space exists, but an
implication is not a rule I have read.

Do this first:

1. Join `discord.gg/allthemods`.
2. Find the channel for server advertising. Likely names: `#server-ads`,
   `#server-advertising`, `#find-a-server`, `#servers`, `#looking-for-server`.
   It may be gated behind a role or an onboarding step.
3. **Read the pinned rules in that channel, completely.**
4. Note the cooldown, any required format or template, and any prohibition
   on links, images, or pings.
5. **Scroll up and read twenty recent posts.** Match their form, not mine.
   A post that looks like the others is read; one that looks different is
   skipped or reported.
6. Write what you find into `research/channels-discord.md` under a "Verified"
   heading with the date.

**Then rewrite the draft below to fit.** What follows is written to the most
common shape of such rules (compact, one message, no pings). If the channel's
actual rules want something different, theirs wins, every time.

One warning worth being blunt about: a large official Discord's advertising
channel is where careless self-promotion gets people banned fastest, and a
ban there would cost the best channel available by a wide margin. Follow the
cooldown exactly. Do not repost because the first one scrolled away. That is
the single most common way people lose access.

## What a good outcome looks like

Channels like this scroll fast, so most posts are seen by relatively few
people despite the headline member count. A realistic good result is 3-10
Discord joins and 1-3 people who actually play. That is still better than
anything else in the plan, which is why it is ranked first.

## Other things to do in that Discord that are not posting

The advertising channel is one post on a cooldown. The rest of the Discord is
59,000 people you can be useful in front of, indefinitely, and this is
worth more over three months than the ad is.

**Answer questions in the support channels.** Somebody is always stuck on
Mekanism, or has a NeoForge crash log, or cannot work out AE2 channels. The
owner can answer a lot of those. Doing so, with a profile that says what it
says, is a far better advertisement than an advertisement.

This is the same mechanism as the Reddit participation rule in
`assets/reddit/comment-engagement.md`, and it matters for the same reason:
it is how a person becomes known as competent, which is the actual
positioning.

**Do not** DM anyone, do not mention the server in channels that are not for
it, and do not answer a support question with a server pitch attached. That
converts a good reputation into a bad one in a single message.
