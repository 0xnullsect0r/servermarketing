# Discord directory listings and bump posts

## Position on bumping, first

**List on Disboard. Do not build a bumping habit around it.**

The mechanics: listing is free, `/bump` promotes the listing to the top of
its tag pages, the cooldown is 2 hours, and **automated bumping is explicitly
prohibited** by Disboard and excluded by the brief's constraints anyway.

Why not to make a habit of it: a 2-hour cooldown is an engine for converting
a one-person operation's scarce attention into a chore. Realistically it
means a few manual bumps a day, competing against servers where a staff rota
bumps twelve times a day, for an audience of Discord-server browsers rather
than modded Minecraft players. That hour a week is worth much more spent in
the ATM Discord.

So: create the listing because it is free and permanent, bump it when you
happen to be at the keyboard and it costs nothing, and **never set a reminder
for it**.

## Where to list

| Directory | Cost | Worth it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Disboard** | Free, `/bump` every 2h | Yes for the listing. No for the treadmill. |
| **Discadia** | Free | Yes, one submission, low effort |
| **Discord Server Discovery** | Free, but has eligibility requirements | Check whether you qualify. Probably not at 49 members, but it costs nothing to look. |
| **top.gg, discord.me** | Free | Only if the submission form is quick. Low expectations. |

I could not read Disboard or top.gg directly (both blocked my requests), so
treat the mechanics above as documented behaviour rather than something I
verified on the page. `QUESTIONS.md` Q9.

## Advertising-Discord posts

Several Discords exist purely for server advertising (`Minecraft LFG ::
Advertise & Find Servers`, `Minecraft Advertisement`, `MC Server
Advertising`, `Modded Minecraft LFG`). I could not resolve their invites to
get member counts, so I cannot rank them honestly and will not pretend to.

**A caution worth stating:** servers whose entire purpose is people
advertising to each other have a structural problem, which is that almost
everyone present is there to post rather than to read. Posting in them is
close to free, so it is worth doing once. Expecting real returns is not
realistic, and anything demanding a recurring bump commitment should be
declined.

The one worth actually finding is **Modded Minecraft LFG**, because "modded"
plus "looking for group" is the right intersection.

Use the draft in `atm-discord-post.md`, shortened. Read each server's
advertising rules first; they typically cap post length and frequency.
